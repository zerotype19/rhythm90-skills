# Rhythm90 Canon (from the book)

Use this as shared background for every Rhythm90 skill. Prefer these truths over generic marketing advice.

## This is not another framework

Frameworks sit in decks, get applause at offsites, and die when quarterly pressure hits. A **rhythm** is a pulse: how a team decides under pressure and moves forward together. When the rhythm works, meetings shorten, emails stop, and the work flows.

## The five choices (Manifesto)

1. **Momentum over motion** — Motion is volume (fifty campaigns, a hundred posts). Momentum is compounding force. Refuse the treadmill.
2. **Signals over stats** — Stats say *what* happened. Signals suggest *why* (friction, resonance, intent). Stats prove labor; signals prove learning.
3. **Clarity over complexity** — A strategy that cannot fit on one page is a distraction, not a strategy.
4. **Consistent learning over heroic launches** — Durable growth comes from test → learn → adapt → repeat, not Super Bowl heroics.
5. **Wisdom over shipping** — A factory ships work. Marketing builds wisdom. End-of-cycle question: not “What did we do?” but “What did we learn?”

## Reject Performance Theater

Reject seventy-slide QBRs, vanity metrics used as camouflage, and the scramble to look busy. Embrace messy discovery: the courage to admit a hypothesis was wrong and pivot.

## Core vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Play** | A deliberate, funded bet with a hypothesis and a signal plan — not a project checklist. |
| **Quarter Board** | Visible scoreboard of 1–3 plays (not a Jira dump). Tracks momentum, not micro-tasks. |
| **Signal Log** | Shared capture of surprises, friction, field feedback, creative response — human nuance dashboards miss. |
| **Kickoff** | Week 1: commit to the plays. |
| **Pulse Check** | ~15 min, slide-free (Weeks 4 & 8): what’s live/stuck, early signals. Never start with “What did everyone do?” Start with “Any surprises?” |
| **Review & Renew (R&R)** | End-of-quarter synthesis into insight — antidote to QBR theater. |
| **Rhythm90 Lead** | Cadence-keeper and shield against theater — not a traditional PM chasing tickets. |

## Learning Engine vs Delivery Machine

Execution still ships campaigns and drives revenue. In a Learning Engine, that execution is the *mechanism* that generates market insight. A campaign is a funded experiment testing a strategic hypothesis.

## Voice (how Rhythm90 sounds)

- Direct, operator-grade, no-nonsense
- Specific over vague; measurable over aspirational
- Call out theater, vanity metrics, and projects-in-disguise
- Prefer short, paste-ready artifacts over essays
- No emoji; no “leverage synergies” filler
