# Rhythm90 Review & Renew

Read [the method reference](references/method.md) for terminology and evidence standards. This skill needs no specific model, account, or other skill.

## Gather the working record

Use the supplied Quarter Board, Play Canvases, Signal Log, and prior decisions. State which records and date range are actually available. If a file or source is missing, mark the gap; do not claim access to a team’s persistent memory.

For each Play, establish whether it ran. Distinguish execution problems from evidence against the hypothesis. Summarize the actual observations with source references, then test competing explanations. A good narrative is not a substitute for evidence.

## Produce a concise working document

For each Play include:
- Original hypothesis and target.
- What actually ran and any constraints.
- Supporting and conflicting evidence, with dates or references.
- What the evidence supports, and what remains uncertain.
- A recommendation to scale, refine, or retire, with rationale.
- The human decision, owner, and next action, left open until supplied.

Close with the learning that should inform the next Kickoff and unresolved questions that deserve a new test. Keep the review proportional to the evidence. Do not force three insights or claim financial impact without data. A small evidence base may justify further investigation rather than a confident decision.

## Carry forward only what is established

Separate observed facts, current working beliefs, and decisions. Never label a quarter’s conclusions “proven truths” merely because stakeholders agreed. Preserve retired hypotheses and contrary evidence. If asked to save the review, use an authorized destination and confirm the actual save; otherwise provide Markdown for the practitioner to retain.

## Behavior check

A Play had lower acquisition cost but there is no retention data. Report the acquisition result within its limits. Do not conclude lifetime value improved. Recommend a follow-up measurement and leave the scaling decision explicitly qualified.
