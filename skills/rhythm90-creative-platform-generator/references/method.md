# Rhythm90: working principles

Rhythm90 is Kevin McGovern’s quarterly marketing practice: choose one to three strategic Plays, capture Signals, run a Kickoff and short Pulse Checks, and close with Review & Renew.

A Play is a deliberate bet about customer behavior, not a delivery checklist. A Signal is an observation that may inform a decision; it is not proof of a causal explanation. The Quarter Board keeps the bets visible. The Signal Log preserves source evidence and interpretation separately. Review & Renew carries learning into the next quarter.

Prefer momentum over motion, signals over statistics alone, clarity over complexity, consistent learning over heroic launches, and learning that changes decisions over output volume. Keep operational KPIs, but do not confuse them with explanations.

Use one to three Plays as a focus constraint, not a reason to invent extra work. Role names can vary by team; identify actual accountability. Keep human decisions distinguishable from AI recommendations.

Source: https://rhythm90.io/dive-into-rhythm90/ (Kevin McGovern). These skills are companion instructions, not verbatim book text.
